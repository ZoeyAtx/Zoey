About me

I like the part where a vague idea slowly turns into something real.

Hai — I’m a freelance developer who likes building useful things, slightly unnecessary things, and occasionally things that start as a terrible idea and somehow end up working.

Most of my work lives somewhere between web development and automation. I like finding repetitive jobs, messy workflows or “surely there’s a better way” problems and turning them into tools that are pleasant to use.

The fun part:

  • figuring out the logic
  • making ugly workflows simple
  • connecting APIs and data
  • building interfaces people actually enjoy using
